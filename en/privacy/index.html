Privacy Policy

Updated November 22, 2026 (cookie consent added). Applies to the page gojira1998.github.io/joaoagents/en/ and to this page.

The AI Agent Office Kit page is run by João Gabriel Alves, trading as JGA AI Services, from Sete Lagoas, Minas Gerais, Brazil. This policy describes exactly what the page does with your data. The kit itself is sold by Gumroad, see "Buying the kit" below.

1. What the kit page loads when you open it

  • Meta Pixel. The page loads the Meta Pixel (Pixel ID 2676980966056593) from connect.facebook.net only after you click Accept in the cookie banner. Until you accept, and if you decline, the Pixel is not loaded and nothing is sent to Meta by this page. If you accept, it reports three events to Meta: PageView and ViewContent (product name "AI Agent Office Kit", value 19, currency USD) when the page loads with your consent, and Lead (label "waitlist-mentorship-en") after you successfully send the waitlist form. The Pixel is used for advertising measurement: to count visits and signups that come from our ads on Facebook and Instagram, to measure results and to build and improve our ad audiences. Meta receives data such as your IP address, browser and device information, the page address and the Meta cookies or identifiers it sets or reads in your browser. We do not choose or control what else Meta collects. Meta's own policies apply to its processing: facebook.com/privacy/policy and facebook.com/privacy/policies/cookies.
  • Google Fonts. The page loads its typeface from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com, so your browser sends a request, including your IP address, to Google.
  • Hosting. The page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep technical logs of visits, such as IP addresses, under its own policy.
  • Video and images. They come from the same site. The video loads only when you press play.
  • Your cookie choice. The page saves your Accept or Decline choice in your browser's local storage (key jga_cookie_consent) so it does not ask again. It stays on your device and is not sent to us. The page sets no other cookie of its own. The only cookies or identifiers besides this come from the Meta Pixel, and only if you accept.

This privacy page itself does not load the Meta Pixel, scripts or fonts from other companies.

2. The waitlist form

The waitlist is for a mentorship about creating with AI that I am preparing. If you send the form, we collect:

  • your name, your email and, if you choose to give it, your WhatsApp or phone number (if you leave it blank, a placeholder number of zeros is stored instead);
  • your answer to "What do you want to learn most?";
  • a record that you ticked the consent box, and a note saying the signup came from this page;
  • where you came from: the value of the utm_source link parameter if there is one, otherwise the website name (not the full address) of the page that sent you here, or "direct".

The form also has a hidden field that only exists to catch spam robots. It is not stored.

Why we use it: to send you updates about the mentorship (dates, seats, details) by email and WhatsApp, because you ticked the consent box. Joining is free and commits you to nothing. Your legal basis, where such laws apply, is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Where it goes: the form sends your data directly from your browser to a hosted database service (Supabase) that we use for signups. It may pass through messaging tools we hire to contact you. We do not sell your data and we do not send it to anyone for their own marketing. We do not ask for health, financial or other sensitive information. Please do not type any in the form.

How long we keep it: until you ask us to delete it or unsubscribe.

The only form event that reaches Meta is the fact that a Lead happened, with the label "waitlist-mentorship-en". Our code does not send your name, email, phone number or answer to the Meta Pixel.

4. Your choices and rights

  • You can ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or withdraw your consent, at any time. Email us at the address below. We reply within 15 days.
  • You can change your cookie choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of the kit page. If you accepted before and now decline, the Pixel stops loading on your next visit, and you can clear the cookies Meta already set in your browser settings.
  • You can also block the Meta Pixel and its cookies with your browser settings or a content blocker, and you can manage Meta ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram account settings. The page still works without the Pixel.
  • To unsubscribe from the mentorship updates, reply to any message we send or email us.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the UK, the EU or a US state with a privacy law), you may have more rights, including the right to complain to your local data protection authority. We honor those rights on request.
